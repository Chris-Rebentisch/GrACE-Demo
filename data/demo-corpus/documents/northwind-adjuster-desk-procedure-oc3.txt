# Northwind Claims Operations — Adjuster Desk Procedure OC-3: Overnight Courier Claims (FICTIONAL)

Owner: Claims Operations (Head of Claims: Priya Nandakumar)
Version: 3, revised 20 March 2026
Audience: overnight courier adjusters and Claims Supervisors

Teaching sample for GrACE-Demo. No real employees, claimants, or claim numbers.

## Roles

- **Overnight courier adjuster** — opens the claim, verifies documents, applies
  the Endorsement C-14 schedule, and closes the claim on the standard path.
- **Claims Supervisor** — the only role that may record the exception rationale
  and approve a payment above the schedule.
- **Head of Claims** — owns this procedure and the exception memo; resolves
  disputes between an adjuster and a supervisor.

## Standard path — step by step

1. Confirm the notice of loss arrived within 48 hours of the scheduled delivery
   window. If it did not, decline under the notice condition and record the
   timestamp evidence.
2. Match the airway bill to the declared contents list. A mismatch is escalated
   to the Claims Supervisor before any settlement.
3. Classify the loss (delay, partial loss or damage, total loss) and apply the
   Endorsement C-14 schedule.
4. Attach the evidence: airway bill, declared contents list, and — for delay
   claims — the carrier's delivery scan history.
5. Settle and close. Standard-path claims do not need supervisor approval.

## When an adjuster believes an exception applies

Do not decide the exception yourself. Send the file to a Claims Supervisor with:

- the carrier delay certificate (weather ground-stop) or the customs hold letter;
- proof that signature-required delivery was requested on the airway bill;
- a one-paragraph note of why the standard schedule is inadequate.

The supervisor records the rationale in the claim file. Only after that
rationale exists may a payment above the schedule be released.

## Evidence rules

Acceptable: carrier delay certificate, customs hold letter, signed delivery
request, carrier delivery scan history. Not acceptable: chat screenshots,
verbal assurances, or an unsigned email from the shipper.
