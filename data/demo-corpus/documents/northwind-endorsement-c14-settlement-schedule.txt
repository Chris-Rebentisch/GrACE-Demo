# Northwind Specialty Cargo — Endorsement C-14: Overnight Courier Settlement Schedule (FICTIONAL)

Endorsement C-14 to the Northwind Specialty Cargo policy form.
Effective: 1 January 2026
Issued by: Northwind Specialty Underwriting, Policy Forms Committee
Applies to: overnight courier shipments declared on the airway bill

This endorsement is a teaching sample for GrACE-Demo. No real policy, insurer,
or policyholder is described.

## 1. Purpose

Endorsement C-14 sets the standard settlement schedule that adjusters apply to
overnight courier claims when no exception applies. The exception process itself
is defined by the Claims Operations exception memo of 12 March 2026, not by this
endorsement.

## 2. Settlement schedule

| Loss category | Standard settlement | Cap per shipment |
|---|---|---|
| Delay beyond the delivery window (no damage) | 25% of declared value | 2,500 |
| Partial loss or damage | Repair or replacement cost, whichever is lower | Declared value |
| Total loss (shipment not delivered) | Declared value | 50,000 |

Amounts are in the policy currency stated on the declarations page.

## 3. Declared value

The declared value is the value stated on the airway bill at the time of
tender. If no value is declared, the schedule uses the invoice value in the
declared contents list, capped at 5,000 per shipment.

## 4. Payments above the schedule

Any payment above the schedule requires (a) the exception conditions in the
Claims Operations exception memo to be met, and (b) a written rationale from a
Claims Supervisor recorded in the claim file before the payment is released.
