# Northwind Overnight — claims exception memo (FICTIONAL)

Internal memo — Claims Operations
Date: 12 March 2026
From: Priya Nandakumar, Head of Claims
To: Overnight courier adjusters

## Purpose

This memo restates the exception process for **overnight courier claims** under
the Northwind Specialty Cargo endorsement. It is a teaching sample for GrACE-Demo.
No real policyholders, claim numbers, or employees are described.

## Standard path

1. Notice of loss must arrive within **48 hours** of the scheduled delivery window.
2. The adjuster verifies the airway bill against the declared contents list.
3. Settlement follows the schedule in Endorsement C-14 unless an exception applies.

## Exception process

An overnight courier claim may leave the standard path only when **all** of the
following are true:

- The delay is caused by a **weather ground-stop** documented by the carrier, or
  by a **customs hold** with a written notice; and
- The shipper requested **signature-required** delivery; and
- A supervisor (role: Claims Supervisor) records a short rationale in the claim
  file **before** any payment above the schedule.

If those conditions are not met, the claim stays on the standard path. Do not
invent a goodwill exception.

## Evidence

Acceptable evidence: carrier delay certificate, customs hold letter, signed
delivery request. Chat screenshots are not evidence.
